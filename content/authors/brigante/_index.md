I am a PhD Student at Sapienza University of Rome, working with the GLADIA research group. Also a musician (I play piano and electric guitar), my research focuses on generative models for audio and music, and representation learning.

In parallel, I work as an Applied AI Researcher at [Translated](https://translated.com) in the Foundation Models team, where I focus on the pre-training, continued pre-training and distillation of large-scale multilingual language models.
